About

Dr. Ambairam Muthu Sivakrishna is an Assistant Professor (Grade II) in the Department of Information Technology at the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal. His research interests include Trustworthy AI and Cybersecurity, neural network theory, machine unlearning, formal verification, and neuro-symbolic and quantum-inspired learning.
